Hi! I'm Sandra. I'm a researcher and activist. I enjoy reading, talking to people, and writing. The topics I explore in my research include sustainability in the Eastern European context, public participation, and ethics. The causes I care about are social justice, migrants' rights, and the fight against eco-apartheid. 

I've been living in Berlin on and off since 2014, and I've been with DCB since 2025. Here, I try to help out however I can within the limits of my available time: organizing events, setting up spaces, and offering my opinion. 

I spend half the year in Romania and the rest of the year in Germany. This means I’m rooted in both Romanian reality and the life of a Berlin-based expat. You might run into me at a modern art gallery in Berlin, in a park in Neukölln, or on a Tuesday at Control. And in my free time, I like to embroider, crochet, or weave.
