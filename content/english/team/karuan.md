Hello! I’m Karuan, and I’ve spent over 10 years navigating Berlin, mostly on the U8 or U7. I’m passionate about cities and their many layers, which are constantly in transition: technological, activist, sustainable, cultural, and so on.
Professionally, I research and help develop urban mobility strategies, from the effects of active mobility on health to the impact of autonomous vehicles on society.
Although I was on the streets at the protests that led to the founding of DCB, I only joined DCB at the beginning of 2026, with the wish to help build a community that is politically engaged, networked, and striving for change.
