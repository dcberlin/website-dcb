Hi, I’m Laura, and I moved to Berlin in early 2025. Since then, I’ve been doing mobile social work at an NGO in the eastern part of the city. I studied fine arts and still identify myself as a visual artist. Ever since high school, I’ve been interested in and actively involved in all kinds of civic projects, and that’s pretty much how I ended up at DCB.
