Casian grew up in Bucharest and lived and studied in the Rhine-Main region for just under ten years before moving to Berlin in 2023 for work – and also for a change of scenery. Whilst still in Darmstadt, he had already been volunteering with a student university group and found a natural fit with the DCB.
Since 2025, he has therefore been trying to contribute to the DCB as much as his time allows: whether by bringing the community closer together, making social and political issues more accessible and raising awareness of them, or creating spaces for interaction and dialogue. For him, social responsibility is a crucial building block in strengthening the community.
